General Surgery call guide

A step-by-step guide for handling inbound General Surgery calls. Scroll down the chart, answer each question, and open a section when a purple box tells you to.

Five rules for every call

  1. Confirm who you are speaking with before you share anything. No match, no PHI.
  2. Check for urgent items first.
  3. Never schedule, cancel or reschedule a surgery yourself. Warm transfer, and send a TE if no one answers.
  4. Never override the on-call schedule when you page.
  5. Before you send a page or TE, check the spelling, the callback number and the GE number.

More detail on the surgery question
  • Warm transfer first. Use the scheduler extension for the patient's surgeon (see the Reference section).
  • Cancelling a surgery, or a call within 3–5 days of surgery: mark the TE High Priority if no one answers.
  • Never schedule, cancel or reschedule a surgery or procedure yourself. If the patient asks to speak with a nurse, send a TE only; do not call the office.

Section 2: Urgent calls and paging

Use this when the patient reports bleeding, vomiting, nausea, fever, severe pain or a wound concern, or when a page may be needed.

New patients: never seen by a Duly surgeon

Do not page a specialty provider who has never seen the patient.

What you can and cannot page for
You can page for
  • Hotwords, if the hotword line is not available
  • Urgent issues and symptoms
  • Questions about a procedure the patient has tomorrow
You do not page for
  • Routine test results
  • Appointment issues
  • Billing or insurance issues
  • Medication refills
  • Non-urgent symptoms that need an appointment
  • Forms
  • Medical advice for non-patients
Scripting when the site or ICC is closed (new patients)
  • Thank the caller for contacting Duly and say you are sorry they are going through this.
  • ICC open: say they are welcome at an ICC location if they feel they need to be seen.
  • After hours: say the ICC is closed and they can use another urgent care or the ER if they feel they need to be seen.
  • Either way: explain you cannot page the provider because they are not an established patient, and offer a new-patient appointment.

Section 4: Office visits

Use this when a patient wants an office visit: new, follow-up, post-op, consult, bariatric, colonoscopy, or a different surgeon.

Insurance rules
  • Traditional Medicaid / HFS: Duly does not participate, except a one-time hospital follow-up with a Duly surgeon or a global requirement.
  • External Medicaid: surgeons do not accept it. Patients on Medicaid cannot be self-pay.
  • BCBS Blue Community: General Surgery sees these patients only when the PCP is assigned with Duly.
  • Self-pay: $150 deposit due at the appointment; the rest is billed.
New consult: provider rules to watch for

Late and cancellation reminders
  • 15-minute grace period. If a patient is running late, call the Reception extension with the ETA.
  • Do not put late notes in the appointment notes.
  • A sooner appointment: book the next available and send a TE to Reception Pool P 12179.

Section 6: Send the page in PerfectServe

Follow these steps every time you page. Never skip a field.

Before you click send: put the patient's GE number first in the Reason for Call, double-check spelling against Epic, and check the callback number.
Page the right person
  • Patient calls and lab critical results: type the provider the patient sees. Never override the call schedule; if someone else is covering, page them.
  • Hospital calls: type the specialty or department name and follow the prompts.
  • You can page by provider name, specialty or practice.
  • If the system asks, choose the on-call provider. Never choose the override option.

Section 7: After you page

Tell the patient what to expect, and know what to do if the page is not answered.

What goes in an RL6
  • MRN
  • Reason for page (medical concern or hotword)
  • Phone number called from
  • Time of page
  • Provider paged
  • Reason for the RL6 (for example unanswered, or multiple pages after 1 hour)
  • Established provider, if different from the provider paged
  • Whether a TE was sent to notify the provider

Open it from the Compass home page: Report an Incident. Questions: riskmanagement@duly.com.
